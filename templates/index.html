🛂 Permanent Residency (PR) & Post-Study Work Permit Guide

Comprehensive regulatory rules, minimum salary thresholds, and points requirements across major education destinations.

🇩🇪

Germany

PR Feasibility: High
  • Post-Study Visa: 18 months Jobseeker permit (Aufenthaltserlaubnis)
  • Work During Study: 140 full days or 280 half days per year
  • Fast-Track PR: EU Blue Card allows PR in just 21 months (with B1 German) or 27 months (with A1/A2 German)
  • STEM Salary Minimum: Reduced threshold ~€41,041/year
  • Visa Proof of Funds: Blocked Account (Sperrkonto) with ~€11,904
🇨🇦

Canada

PR Feasibility: Moderate
  • Post-Study Visa: 3-year PGWP (Master's and PhD graduates qualify regardless of length)
  • Work During Study: 24 hours per week off-campus
  • PR Pathway: Express Entry (CEC), Provincial Nominee Programs (PNP: Ontario, BC Tech)
  • Advantage: Category-based STEM & Healthcare draws have lower CRS cutoffs
  • Visa Proof of Funds: $20,635 CAD living funds + 1st year tuition
🇺🇸

United States

PR Feasibility: Challenging
  • Post-Study Visa: 12 months OPT + 24 months STEM OPT Extension (36 months total)
  • Work During Study: 20 hours/week on-campus; CPT for internships
  • PR Pathway: H-1B Specialty Occupation lottery (20,000 master's exemption cap) -> EB-2 / EB-3 Green Card or EB-2 NIW for PhD researchers
  • Advantage: Highest post-grad tech & finance compensation in the world ($110k-$160k+)
🇬🇧

United Kingdom

PR Feasibility: Moderate
  • Post-Study Visa: 2-year Graduate Route Visa (3 years for PhD)
  • Work During Study: 20 hours per week during term time
  • PR Pathway: Switch to Skilled Worker Visa -> Indefinite Leave to Remain (ILR) after 5 years
  • Salary Threshold: Standard £38,700/yr (new entrant discount for students under 26: ~£30,960)
  • Advantage: 1-year master's programs reduce opportunity cost and time-to-work
🇦🇺

Australia

PR Feasibility: Moderate
  • Post-Study Visa: 2 to 4 years Subclass 485 Temporary Graduate Visa
  • Work During Study: 48 hours per fortnight
  • PR Pathway: General Skilled Migration: Subclass 189, 190, 491
  • Advantage: Regional study grants 5 extra PR points and extended work rights
🇳🇱

Netherlands

PR Feasibility: High
  • Post-Study Visa: 1-year Orientation Year (Zoekjaar) visa for top graduates
  • Work During Study: 16 hours/week with work permit
  • PR Pathway: Highly Skilled Migrant (Kennismigrant) permit -> Dutch PR after 5 years
  • Advantage: Lowered salary requirement (€2,801/mo) for Zoekjaar alumni, virtually 100% English fluency
